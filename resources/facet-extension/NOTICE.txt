Facet – filter lists and licenses

The ad, tracker and malware blocking in this extension is compiled from the filter lists that
uBlock Origin (https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock) enables by default. The original lists are
included in third-party/lists/, with their source URL, download date and SHA-256 in
third-party/sources.json.

  uBlock filters (Ads, Badware risks, Privacy, Unbreak, Quick fixes)
    The uBlock Origin authors – https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uAssets
    License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (third-party/GPL-3.0.txt)

  EasyList, EasyPrivacy
    The EasyList authors – https://easylist.to/
    License: GNU GPL v3.0 or Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
    (third-party/GPL-3.0.txt, third-party/CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt)

  Peter Lowe's Ad and tracking server list
    Peter Lowe – https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/
    Terms: free to combine with other lists and publish, with attribution.

  Online Malicious URL Blocklist (hosts variant)
    malware-filter – https://gitlab.com/malware-filter/urlhaus-filter
    Data: URLhaus by abuse.ch (CC0 1.0); list: CC0 1.0 and MIT.

Derived files: rules/ads.json, rules/privacy.json, rules/malware.json, rules/unbreak.json and
cosmetic.json are generated from the lists above and are distributed under the GNU GPL v3.0
(the license of the uBlock filters), together with the lists themselves.

Modifications by Facet: network filters are converted to Chromium declarativeNetRequest rules
(blocking, exceptions, important, domain, third-party, resource types, denyallow, to, method and
badfilter), with whole-host filters grouped by domain. Element hiding filters become per-site
and generic CSS selectors, applied as a user style sheet. Filters that require changing the
page's JavaScript or HTML (scriptlets, procedural and extended CSS, HTML filters), redirects,
query parameter removal, CSP injection, popup blocking and regular expressions are not
included. The URLhaus list is the variant with whole malicious hosts. This is a subset of the
original filter lists, not a complete implementation, and no endorsement by their authors is
implied.

Regenerate from the included lists (offline), or download them again:
  node scripts/build-facet-filters.mjs
  node scripts/build-facet-filters.mjs --update
The extension does not download executable code or filter updates at runtime.
